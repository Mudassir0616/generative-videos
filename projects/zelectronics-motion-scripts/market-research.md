## 2. What this means for ZELECTRONICS' messaging
- **The timing is right.** New prices are rising for a reason buyers can understand (AI is eating the memory supply), and the price gap to refurbished is widening. That one fact backs a whole series of videos.
- **The best audience is businesses: startups, SMEs and offices.** They are the fastest-growing PC buyers (+21%), the largest group buying refurbished, and the audience the ZELECTRONICS intro already speaks to ("IT for startups & SMEs", bulk pricing, SLA-backed support).
- **Desktops matter as much as laptops** for SME offices. Keep "laptops and desktops" in every CTA.
- **Windows 10's end of support is a concrete hook for businesses.** Offices still on old PCs face a security risk, and Windows 11-ready refurbished machines solve it without new prices.
- **Trust is the barrier.** Testing, grading, warranty and honest advice are what turn "used" into "certified refurbished". ZELECTRONICS must supply the real details, because the scripts can't invent them.
- **Sustainability is a supporting reason, not the lead.** Indian buyers act on price first. E-waste works as a secondary line or as a separate CSR/LinkedIn piece.

## 3. ZELECTRONICS must confirm before any of these go out
- **Certification:** who certifies the machines, and what testing or grading is done (number of checks, battery health threshold, drive health, cosmetic grades)?
- **Warranty:** six months, and every laptop is tested before it ships (both from the user, 2026-11-12). Still to confirm: what the SLA covers for businesses, and whether desktops get the same warranty.
- **Typical saving** against the same model new (a % or a ₹ example).
- **Windows 11 ready:** is the stock Windows 11 compatible, and does it ship with a licensed OS?
- **Bulk/office offer:** minimum order, free consultation, delivery time in Mumbai, and whether it delivers across India.
- **Real price examples** for script 3 (the ten-desk maths).
