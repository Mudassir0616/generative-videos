## Claims to verify before publishing

- **"Memory prices have more than doubled"**: supported. IDC India says RAM is up 2.5–3× (Business Today, June 2026); TrendForce reports quarter-on-quarter DRAM contract jumps of 55–63% in H1 2026.
- **"New laptops are expensive" / "costs more than ever"**: supported. Dell, Lenovo and HP raised prices 15–20%; Indian laptop prices are up roughly 10–12% so far (IDC).
- **"Tested", "Certified", "Backed by warranty", "A fraction of the price"**: **ZELECTRONICS must confirm these.** Who certifies the laptops, how long the warranty is, and the typical saving.
