## Pacing
- Hard cuts on the beat. Ref 1 cut list: 0.73, 1.40, 2.00, 2.53, 3.03, 3.50, 3.97, 4.37, 4.73, 5.17 ... 8.90. Shots shrink from about 0.67s to 0.3s (accelerating).
- Ref 4 cuts every 0.27s (8th notes at about 110 BPM). Ref 2 changes scene about every 1.5-3s but always has something moving.
- Music about 108-120 BPM. Cuts land on beats or 8ths.

## Type
- Lowercase geometric/grotesk sans (Proxima Nova / Gilroy feel), bold, tight tracking. Free match: **Figtree** ExtraBold/Black.
- Stacked size contrast: a small word sits on a big word ("we / don't", "just / edit", "don't / need / content.").
- Accent words in **italic serif** (ref 2: "every second", "spends", "Aren't loud"). Free match: **Instrument Serif Italic**.
- Text usually centred, about 100-250px at 1080 wide.

## Text animation (all fast, 0.15-0.35s)
- Blur-in: the word starts big and blurred, then snaps sharp and settles.
- Liquid warp: letters ribbon in with a wavy distortion.
- Typewriter with a thin cursor bar.
- Light sweep or glow pass across a word.
- Words added in place ("they" becomes "they need").
- Rare scene exits: mostly hard cuts.

## Background / motion graphics (never an empty frame)
- Thin circles and arcs, rotating dial with tick marks, crop-mark corner frames.
- Tiny annotation/UI text in corners (poster/editorial detail), thin rules.
- Giant cropped background word, blurred or low-contrast, drifting sideways.
- Film grain / dust specks on flat colour.
- Infographic pieces: counters in rings, nodes with connector lines, pill chips stacking (filled vs outline).

## Colour
- Ref 1: pure black/white. Refs 2-4: black/white plus ONE saturated accent.

## Sound
- Driving electronic beat, cuts on the grid; whooshes or ticks on transitions; loudness about -17 dBFS RMS.
