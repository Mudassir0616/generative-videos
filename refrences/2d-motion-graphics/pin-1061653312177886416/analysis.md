## Motion language
- **One transition grammar: blur.** Almost every change is a defocus: the outgoing scene blurs and fades for 0.13–0.2 s, the incoming one starts blurred and snaps into focus in 0.15–0.3 s. Big moments get a stronger variant: zoom-through into an extreme close-up (2→3), spin-whip with rotational blur (4→5), light-orb wipe to white (6→7), gravity drop (10→11), light-leak burn (14→15). No slides or pushes between scenes and no hard cuts except the cover frame.
- **Text entrance = blur-to-sharp + fade + small rise.** Every word or line starts at about 0% opacity with heavy Gaussian blur (roughly 10–15 px at 640 wide) and about 10–15 px below its rest position, reaching full opacity and focus in 0.15–0.35 s (lead-ins 0.13 s, big key words about 0.3 s). Strong ease-out, no overshoot or bounce. Words never leave individually: the whole scene blurs out together.
- **Stagger:** lead-in → key word → sub-line, 0.1–0.25 s apart. In the list and the "When those are unclear" scene the stagger follows the VO word by word.
- **VO sync:** the scene changes in the VO gap before each phrase (2.83, 4.97, 8.27, 12.87, 15.87, 18.2, 22.8, 24.5, 27.23, 28.95, 30.5). Short words land 0.0–0.2 s before they are spoken; the big grey key words of long phrases land well before them (0.4–1.0 s: "Agencies", "Category", "The problem"), so the full sentence is readable while it is being said.
- **Constant life during holds:** chrome corner stars rotate, CRT spinner loops, "Slide to answer" shimmer sweeps, ✦ bullets rotate, scenes drift a few pixels. Nothing is ever completely still.
- **Persistent elements bridge scenes:** logo, vignette, grid and corner stars never change across 31 s, and the blue pin stays fixed through three keyword scenes, only blurring between them, so the keywords read as one list.
- **Metaphor per line:** paper boats = "the same" agencies, two walkers = different category, thinker statue = deliverables/output, monitor-head meditator = busywork metrics, chess on social icons = strategy above platforms, boardroom = inside the business, pin = key point, handshake = ownership, falling man = marketing that fails, eyeball = "exposes it".

## Recreate-it notes (HyperFrames)
- 1080x1920, 30 fps. Build one **background layer** for the whole piece: off-white #FDFBF7 with a radial vignette to about #E2E0DC at the edges, a faint dashed grid (about 120 px cells at 1080 wide, 1 px dashed lines at 6–8% black), the logo fixed top-centre, and two chrome corner shapes (PNG/video of a metal star, or an SVG with a metallic gradient) slowly rotating over a 4–5 s loop.
- Scenes are `<div>`s with a greyscale cut-out (`filter: grayscale(1)` plus `drop-shadow` for the contact shadow) and a text stack. Text entrance per element: `from { opacity:0; filter:blur(24px); transform:translateY(24px) } to { opacity:1; filter:blur(0); transform:none }`, 0.25–0.5 s, `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` (expo-out). Scene out: animate the whole scene to `filter: blur(30px); opacity:0` in 0.15–0.2 s; scene in: from `blur(30px); opacity:0; scale(1.08)` to sharp in 0.2–0.3 s.
- Special transitions: zoom-through = next image starts at `scale(3)` + blur and scales to 1 in 0.4 s while crossfading; spin-whip = `rotate(35deg) translate(15%,20%)` + blur + fade over 0.35 s; light orb = a white radial-gradient circle scaling from 0 to 3x over 0.35 s; gravity drop = `rotate(25deg) translateY(120%)` with ease-in and blur, 0.3 s; a falling object enters with `translateY(-120%)` + motion blur and ease-out.
- Two-tone hierarchy: black key word, grey (#74736F) accent word, small regular lead-in at about one third of the key size.
- Timing: use `vo_words.json`-style word times for your VO, start each scene in the gap before its phrase, bring short words in 0.1–0.2 s before they are spoken and long-sentence key words up to about 0.8 s early. Whoosh SFX only on the big transitions.
- End card: dark #202020 plate, logo reveal from fragments (clip-path or masked strokes left to right over 0.8 s), URL at the bottom, fade to black over the last second.
